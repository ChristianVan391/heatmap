# Fluid Thermal Pad 🔥
An interactive web toy where you paint glowing heat maps with your mouse or finger.

## What you can do
- Draw: Click and drag to create smooth, flowing heat trails.

- Themes: Swap color palettes (thermal, rainbow, stripy, and more) from the menu.

- Tweak settings: Adjust how fast the heat fades or change your brush size.

- Pause: Freeze the canvas to keep your drawing in place.
